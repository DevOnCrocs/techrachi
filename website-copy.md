## PART 1: PAGE ARCHITECTURE

### Goal and audience
- **Primary conversion:** Book a free 30-minute Strategy Call.
- **Secondary conversion:** Try the live agent demo (low-commitment micro-conversion that warms visitors toward the call).
- **Audience:** SME owners, operations leads, and founders who are drowning in repetitive work, missing calls and leads, and paying for headcount that doesn't scale.
- **Core message:** *We build AI that does the work, so your team can grow the business.*

### Section order and the job each section does

| # | Section | Job to be done | Psychology lever | CTA |
|---|---------|----------------|------------------|-----|
| 0 | Sticky Nav | Orientation, always-visible conversion | Reduce friction | "Book a Strategy Call" |
| 1 | Hero | Say what we do, for whom, and the payoff in 5 seconds | Clarity, specificity, loss framing | Primary + Secondary |
| 2 | Trust / Metrics Bar | Remove "is this real?" doubt immediately | Social proof, authority | None (passive) |
| 3 | Problem Agitation (short) | Make the cost of inaction felt | Loss aversion, status-quo bias | None (flows into services) |
| 4 | Core Services Grid (4 cards) | Let visitors self-identify their need | Choice simplification, outcome framing | "See how it works" per card |
| 5 | How It Works + Live Demo | Prove it works; make AI tangible | Demonstration, IKEA effect, reduced perceived risk | "Try the demo" → "Book a call" |
| 6 | ROI Comparison | Make the business case in numbers | Anchoring, contrast effect | "Calculate your savings" / Book |
| 7 | Engagement Models | Let buyers pick a lane without sticker shock | Decoy/anchor tiers, risk reversal | Per-tier CTA |
| 8 | FAQ | Kill objections right before the ask | Objection handling | None |
| 9 | Final CTA + Footer | Convert the undecided | Urgency (honest), risk reversal | "Book Your Free Strategy Call" |

### Navigation (anchor links)
`Services` · `How It Works` · `ROI` · `Pricing` · `FAQ` · **[Book a Call]** (button)

### CTA strategy
- **One primary CTA repeated** (Book a Strategy Call): Nav, Hero, post-Demo, Pricing, Final CTA.
- **One secondary CTA** (Try the Live Demo): Hero and Services.
- Every CTA button opens the same booking flow (calendar embed or short form: name, email, business type, biggest bottleneck).

### Recommended future pages (not v1)
`/services/ai-chatbots` · `/services/workflow-agents` · `/services/voice-agents` · `/services/web-development` · `/case-studies` · `/about` · `/contact`. Each service card in Section 4 should deep-link to its page once built; for v1 they scroll to the demo.
